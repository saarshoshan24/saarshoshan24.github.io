Email-to-Deal Tracker

Automated Sales Pipeline, Follow-Up Nudges & Executive KPI Digest

Bottleneck
A B2B supplier emailed PDF price quotes to customers, then lost track of which ones salespeople actually followed up on.
Automation
Built an app that polls the quote mailbox via IMAP, extracts customer & amount data from each PDF quote, assigns the right salesperson in SQLite, sends 4-day follow-up reminders, and emails the manager a Sunday KPI scorecard.
Impact
Zero change to how the manager sends quotes. Mobile-first Hebrew RTL interface used every day by the sales team and management.

Mark Clipper

Side-Panel Web Workbench & Workflow Engine for AI Content Operations

Bottleneck
Copying web articles, tables, and research into AI assistants drags in noisy DOM clutter, breaks formatting, and exposes prompts to hidden injection text.
Automation
Engineered a docked Chrome MV3 side panel that auto-follows the active tab, extracts clean Markdown, tables, and JSON-LD/OpenGraph metadata, and runs saved multi-step capture-to-AI workflows in one click.
Security
Strips hidden DOM nodes and invisible Unicode tags, wraps untrusted content in <document> context packs, and operates 100% locally with zero network calls or API keys.
