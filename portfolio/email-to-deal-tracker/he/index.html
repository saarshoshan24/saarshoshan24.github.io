No price quote gets forgotten.

I built an app that turns every quote email into a deal for the right salesperson, reminds them to follow up, and sends the manager a weekly summary. Nobody had to change how they work, and it has run their sales follow-up every day since July 2026.

    How it works

    Fits the way the team already works

    Nobody had to change habits. The app works around the email the manager was already sending.

    1. 01A quote goes outThe manager emails a price quote, same as always.
    2. 02It becomes a dealThe app picks it up, reads the customer and amount, and assigns the right salesperson.
    3. 03A timely reminderFour days later the salesperson gets a reminder that links straight to the deal.
    4. 04One tap to updateClosed, needs follow-up, or cancelled. Cancellations alert the manager right away.
    5. 05A weekly summaryEvery Sunday the manager gets each salesperson's numbers in one email.

    In production

    Problems I solved after launch

    Deals that silently went missing

    Problem
    A few quote emails never became deals, and nothing showed an error.
    What I did
    Traced it to how the app decided which emails were new, and rebuilt that step so an email can't be skipped.

    Reminders that never arrived

    Problem
    The app reported reminders as sent, but a salesperson never got them.
    What I did
    Tested step by step until I found the recipient's company mail filter holding them, instead of trusting the green checkmark.

    Recovering after data loss

    Problem
    The office installation was wiped.
    What I did
    Rebuilt every deal from the mailbox, then caught that their reminders weren't switched on and fixed that too.

    Design from real feedback

    Problem
    The manager missed notes he had written, and deals blurred together in the list.
    What I did
    Watched how he actually used it and redesigned both. Every screen here went through rounds like that.
